02 /

Spellforge Lab is an independent studio based in France, created and run by a couple combining development and graphic design.

The studio creates games, interactive experiences and practical tools for developers. We explore projects that combine creativity, usability and technical experimentation, with a strong interest in building distinctive experiences on a small, independent scale.
